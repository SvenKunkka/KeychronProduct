## 核心卖点

- 全尺寸104键布局，配备12个多媒体键，办公效率直接拉满 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- Keychron Super机械轴体，手感顺滑，适合长时间打字 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 三模连接（2.4G/蓝牙/有线），多设备无缝切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4 GHz / 蓝牙5.3 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 回报率：蓝牙模式125Hz，支持无线和有线全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 键帽：双色注塑PBT，Cherry高度，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，关背光续航约310小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 背光：22种RGB灯效，可自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 打字角度：2.37º和5.33º，两段式脚撑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
- 工作环境：-10至50℃ [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k240-r6-source.md)
