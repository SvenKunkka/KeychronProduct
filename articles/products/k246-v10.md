## 产品定位

为办公与入门用户打造的 75% 配列有线机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 办公用户：需要稳定可靠、手感舒适的有线机械键盘，提升打字效率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 入门机械键盘用户：首次尝试热插拔、RGB 等可玩性功能，预算有限 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 桌面整洁爱好者：75% 配列节省空间，搭配简约桌搭 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 75% 配列 + Tray Mount 结构：紧凑布局兼顾功能与手感，办公游戏两相宜 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 热插拔轴座（兼容 5 针/3 针）：轻松换轴，体验不同手感，入门客制化首选 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- South-facing RGB + OSA 键帽：灯光效果更佳，键帽兼容性高 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：Type-C 有线连接（附 Type-A 转接头） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 回报率：1000 Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 配列：75% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron K Pro（热插拔，兼容 5 pin & 3 pin） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 键帽：Double-shot PBT 键帽，不透光，OSA 高度（OEM 高度，SA 形状） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 背光：South-facing RGB LED [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 卫星轴：Screw-in PCB 卫星轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 主控：Ultra-low-power ARM Cortex-M4 32-bit STM32L432（128KB Flash） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 外壳材质：ABS 塑料 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 定位板材质：钢制定位板 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 安装结构：Tray Mount 设计 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 尺寸：386.7×141.3×27.2 mm（前高 18.7 mm，不含键帽） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 重量：1230±10 g（整装版） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 倾斜角度：3.5° / 6.7° / 9.3°（脚垫高度 3 mm） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
- 工作环境：-10℃ 至 50℃ [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k246-v10-source.md)
