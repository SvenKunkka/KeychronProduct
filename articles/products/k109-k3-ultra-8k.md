## 产品定位

75% 低矮型三模 8K 机械键盘。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 75% 低矮配列，支持 ZMK 与 Keychron Launcher [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)
- 三模连接：2.4 GHz / Bluetooth 5.2 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)
- 有线与 2.4 GHz 模式支持 8000 Hz 回报率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4 GHz / Bluetooth 5.2 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)

- 回报率：8000 Hz（有线与 2.4 GHz）；125 Hz（Bluetooth） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k109-k3-ultra-8k-source.md)
