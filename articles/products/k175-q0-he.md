## 核心卖点

- 0.1mm 快速触发，0.2-3.8mm 可调触发行程，电竞级响应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 三模连接（2.4G/蓝牙/有线），多设备无缝切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 全铝机身，CNC 工艺，桌面质感拉满 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4 GHz / Bluetooth / Type-C wired [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 回报率：1000 Hz（有线及 2.4G），90 Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 配列：独立数字小键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：137 x 118.17mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron Double-rail Magnetic Switch，热插拔（仅兼容 Gateron 双轨磁轴） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 键帽：Cherry 高度，侧刻字符 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 电池：1800 mAh，续航最高 56 小时（背光关闭） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK / VIA，可自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- RGB：South-facing RGB LED，支持多种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 声学填充：多层消音结构，优化敲击音 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k175-q0-he-source.md)
