## 产品定位

面向游戏场景的 35 键单手机械键盘，采用磁轴和 8000 Hz 有线回报率。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 35 键单手布局 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)
- 8000 Hz 回报率，支持在 Launcher 中切换 1000 / 4000 / 8000 Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)
- 单键扫描率最高 256 kHz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)
- Keychron Ultra-fast Lime 磁轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)
- 不可拆卸 USB-A 纯有线连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k018-c0-he-source.md)
