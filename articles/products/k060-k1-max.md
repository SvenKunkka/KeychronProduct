## 产品定位

## 目标用户

- 追求轻薄便携与桌面整洁的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 需要多设备切换的跨平台用户（macOS/Windows/Linux） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 轻度游戏玩家，需要低延迟无线连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 机械键盘入门用户，希望体验矮轴手感 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 87键80%紧凑布局，保留完整功能键区，节省桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- Gateron矮轴+矮键帽设计，总高度仅7.72mm，带来轻薄手感与便携性 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 2.4GHz+蓝牙+有线三模连接，无线/有线均支持全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.1 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 蓝牙模式回报率90Hz，有线模式更高 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 2000mAh锂电池，关背光续航约95小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 22种RGB背光模式，支持自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 低剖面双色注塑PBT键帽，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 支持macOS/Windows/Linux，附赠Mac/Win键帽 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 12个多媒体按键，提升操作效率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 3.37°/4.2°/6.7°三档脚撑，可调打字角度 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
- 工作温度-10~50℃，适应多种环境 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k060-k1-max-source.md)
