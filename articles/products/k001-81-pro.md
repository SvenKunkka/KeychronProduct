## 产品定位

将全CNC铝合金机身、蓝牙5.1与4000mAh长续航结合在一起的81键紧凑型机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求桌面质感与精致工艺的机械键盘发烧友 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 需要多设备切换的办公用户（Mac/Windows/Linux） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 注重无线续航与便携性的移动办公人群 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 喜欢自定义灯光与键帽的DIY玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 全CNC铝合金机身：整块铝材精密加工，质感与强度兼备，搭配PC定位板，兼顾手感与声音表现 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 4000mAh大电池：关背光续航高达300小时，开RGB也能坚持100小时，无线使用告别电量焦虑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 蓝牙5.1多设备连接：支持Mac/Windows/Linux/Android/iOS，一键切换，跨平台无缝办公 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.1 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 回报率：有线1000Hz，无线90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 配列：81键ANSI（US）全组装，紧凑布局 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)

- 键帽：Double Shot PBT（非透光），耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 卫星轴：螺丝式PCB卫星轴，稳定不晃动 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 背光：South-facing RGB LED，支持自定义灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 热插拔：支持，方便更换轴体 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)

- 无冲：无线/有线均支持全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh锂聚合物电池，关背光续航300小时，开RGB（最低亮度）100小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 系统兼容：Mac/Windows/Linux/Android/iOS [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 重量：1887±20g，稳固扎实 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
- 尺寸：33.83×15.05×4.73cm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k001-81-pro-source.md)
