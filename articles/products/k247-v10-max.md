## 核心卖点

- 三模连接，2.4G/蓝牙/有线自由切换，4000mAh大电池，关背光续航长达190小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- Gasket Mount结构配合PC定位板，手感软弹，声音纯净 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 全键热插拔，兼容主流3针/5针MX轴体，轻松DIY [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.1 / 2.4GHz / USB-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 回报率：1000Hz（2.4G/有线），90Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 配列：75%布局，紧凑实用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron Jupiter（整装版） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 键帽：Double-shot PBT，OSA高度，不透光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，充电约5小时，关背光续航190小时，开RGB最低亮度100小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 软件：QMK/VIA改键（主控STM32F402） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- RGB：南向RGB背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 声学：Gasket Mount + PC定位板，填充优化 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
- 系统：Windows/macOS/Linux，支持Mac/Win双系统 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k247-v10-max-source.md)
