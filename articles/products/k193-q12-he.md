## 核心卖点

- 快速触发与可调触发行程：0.2-3.8mm，灵敏度0.1mm，带来毫秒级响应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 4-in-1动态按键：一键实现四重功能，提升操作效率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 全铝机身：铝制外壳与定位板，提供坚实手感与高级质感 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.2 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 回报率：1000Hz（有线和2.4GHz）/ 90Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 配列：96% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron双轨磁轴，热插拔（兼容Gateron双轨磁轴） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 键帽：OSA双色PBT，不透光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，续航最高100小时（关背光） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 背光：South-facing RGB [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 主控：ARM Cortex-M4 STM32F402 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 卫星轴：螺丝固定PCB卫星轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 系统：Mac/Win双系统支持 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
- 软件：QMK/VIA支持 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k193-q12-he-source.md)
