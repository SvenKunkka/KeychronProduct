## 核心卖点

- 全CNC铝制外壳，表面涂层处理，质感与耐用性兼备 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)
- 钢制定位板，提供坚实敲击手感和稳定声音表现 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)
- 60%紧凑配列，释放桌面空间，专注核心输入体验 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)

- 键帽：Cherry 高度 PBT 双色注塑键帽 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)

- 主控：Ultra-low-power ARM Cortex-M4 32-bit STM32L432 (128KB Flash) [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)
- 兼容：Mac/Win 双系统 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k229-q60-source.md)
