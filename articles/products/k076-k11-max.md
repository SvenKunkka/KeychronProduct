## 产品定位

把矮轴手感、三模连接和紧凑 65% 配列结合在一起的便携型键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求便携与桌面整洁的移动办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- Mac/iPad 多设备切换的轻量用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 对机械手感有要求但偏好矮轴的低矮键盘爱好者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Gateron 矮轴 + 低剖面双色 PBT 键帽，兼顾轻薄与机械手感 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 三模连接（2.4G/蓝牙/有线），蓝牙 5.1 支持多设备无缝切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 2000mAh 电池，关背光续航约 95 小时，满足一周以上重度使用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙 5.1 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 蓝牙回报率：90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 配列：68 键 65% ANSI 布局，支持全键无冲（无线/有线） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron 矮轴（North-facing） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 键帽：低剖面双色 PBT，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 电池：2000mAh 锂聚合物电池，关背光续航约 95 小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 背光：22 种 RGB 灯效，支持自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 尺寸：346×115mm，重量 520g，前高 10.6mm，后高 15.6mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 打字角度：3.33°/4.7°/7.54° 三档可调 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
- 工作温度：-10~50℃ [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k076-k11-max-source.md)
