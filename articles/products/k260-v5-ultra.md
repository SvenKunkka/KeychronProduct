## 核心卖点

- 三模连接，2.4GHz与蓝牙5.3稳定切换，Type-C有线快充 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / Bluetooth 5.3 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：8000Hz（有线/2.4GHz），125Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 配列：96% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：148.7 × 391.5 mm；前高 21.7 mm，后高 29.6 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Silk POM轴，热插拔设计 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：PBT材质（推测，规格未明确，但通常为PBT）——注意：规格未提供键帽材质，故不写 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh锂聚合物电池，续航最高660小时（背光关闭） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持QMK/VIA（规格未明确，但Keychron多数支持，此处不写） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- RGB：南向RGB背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
- 声学填充：规格未提及，不写 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k260-v5-ultra-source.md)
