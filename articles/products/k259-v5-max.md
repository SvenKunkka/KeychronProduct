## 产品定位

为办公与多设备用户打造的96%配列三模无线机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求全尺寸布局与数字小键盘效率的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 需要三模连接与多设备切换的桌面工作者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 注重键盘手感与耐用性的机械键盘入门及进阶用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 需要跨平台兼容（Windows/macOS/Linux）的开发者与IT人员 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 96%配列融合数字小键盘与紧凑布局，节省桌面空间同时保留全功能输入效率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- Gasket Mount结构搭配PC定位板，提供柔和一致的敲击手感与悦耳声音反馈 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 4000mAh大电池，关背光续航长达190小时，开RGB也能坚持100小时（最低亮度） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 三模连接：蓝牙5.1 / 2.4GHz / 有线USB-C，支持Windows/macOS/Linux系统 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 1000Hz回报率（2.4GHz及有线模式），蓝牙模式90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- Gateron Jupiter轴体，热插拔兼容3针/5针MX轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- Double-shot PBT键帽，OSA高度（OEM高度+SA形状），不透明 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 4000mAh锂电池，充电时间约5小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- ARM Cortex-M4 32-bit STM32F402主控，支持QMK/VIA改键 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- South-facing RGB背光，支持自定义灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- Screw-in PCB卫星轴，提升大键稳定性 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- Gasket Mount设计，内部填充优化声音 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
- 无冲模式（无线及有线均支持） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k259-v5-max-source.md)
