- Tray Mount 结构，手感稳定一致 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- Keychron K Pro 轴体，出厂润滑，顺滑耐用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)

- 连接方式：Type-C 有线连接，附带 Type-C 转 Type-A 转接头 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 回报率：1000 Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 配列：80%（TKL），紧凑节省桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron K Pro 轴，支持热插拔（5 脚和 3 脚） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 键帽：OEM 高度双色注塑 PBT 键帽，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 背光：South-facing RGB LED [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 卫星轴：钢板卫星轴，大键更稳定 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 外壳：ABS 塑料，轻量化设计（约 700g） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 倾斜角度：4.8° / 8.5° / 10.7°，三档可调 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
- 系统兼容：Mac/Win 双系统，附赠适配键帽 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k020-c1-pro-source.md)
