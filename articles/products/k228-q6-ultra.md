## 核心卖点

- 8000Hz 回报率：有线与 2.4GHz 模式下均支持 8000Hz，电竞级响应速度，操作更跟手 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- CNC 铝合金外壳：全铝机身，重达 2238g，稳如磐石，敲击时纹丝不动 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 4000mAh 大电池：蓝牙关闭背光续航长达 660 小时，RGB 最低亮度也能用 200 小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙 5.3 / Type-C 有线，三模连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：有线与 2.4GHz 均 8000Hz，蓝牙 125Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 配列：全尺寸 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：137 × 446 mm；前高 20.6 mm，后高 31.09 mm；倾斜角 5.3° [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Silk POM 轴，出厂润滑，顺滑耐用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：PBT 材质（默认），支持热插拔 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh 锂聚合物电池 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK / VIA，可自定义键位和灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- RGB：South-facing RGB LED，灯效均匀 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 声学填充：内置消音棉，敲击声更纯净 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux 全兼容 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
- 其他：螺丝固定 PCB 卫星轴，稳定不晃 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k228-q6-ultra-source.md)
