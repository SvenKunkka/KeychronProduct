## 产品定位

融合磁轴性能、三模连接与铝坨坨质感的100%配列电竞键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求极致响应速度的硬核电竞玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 需要全尺寸布局与高效办公的专业人士 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 注重桌面质感与耐用性的机械键盘发烧友 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 对键盘可玩性和自定义有深度需求的高级用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Gateron双轨磁轴，支持0.1mm灵敏度与0.2-3.8mm可调触发行程，实现快速触发与动态按键（4合1动作键） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 全铝机身（外壳+定位板），重达2178g，提供稳固底座与扎实手感 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 三模连接（2.4G/蓝牙/Type-C），无线模式同样支持1000Hz回报率与全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.2 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 回报率：1000Hz（有线/2.4G），90Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 配列：100% 全尺寸 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：137 × 446 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron双轨磁轴，热插拔（仅兼容同款） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 键帽：OSA Double-shot PBT，不透光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，关背光续航约100小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 软件：支持QMK（主控STM32F402RC） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- RGB：South-facing，可自定义灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 声学：Screw-in PCB卫星轴，填充结构 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
- 系统：兼容Mac/Win [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k224-q6-he-source.md)
