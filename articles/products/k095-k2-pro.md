## 产品定位

把紧凑75%配列、双模连接与舒适打字体验结合在一起的办公型机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求高效办公与多设备切换的Mac用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 喜欢紧凑布局但需要完整功能键的桌面工作者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 注重性价比的机械键盘入门与进阶玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 需要无线自由与有线高性能的双模用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 75%配列，84键紧凑布局，保留F键区与方向键，节省桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 双模连接（蓝牙5.1+Type-C），支持3设备切换，办公效率倍增 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 4000mAh大电池，关背光续航约260小时，开RGB约130小时，告别电量焦虑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.1 + Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 回报率：有线1000Hz，无线90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 配列：ANSI，75%，84键 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 轴体：热插拔，南向RGB，兼容3脚/5脚轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 键帽：OSA高度（OEM球面角度），PBT材质 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh锂聚合物电池 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 软件：QMK/VIA全键自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- RGB：22种背光模式，可自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 声学：PCB螺丝固定卫星轴，钢制定位板，声音更扎实 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
- 系统：macOS/Windows/Linux全兼容 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k095-k2-pro-source.md)
