## 产品定位

更薄、更轻、更高效的80%配列无线机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求桌面整洁与便携性的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 需要多设备切换的Mac/Windows双系统用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 轻度游戏玩家，偏好低轴体高度带来的快速响应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 对键盘手感有要求但不想牺牲桌面空间的用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 超薄铝合金边框设计，前端高度仅7.72mm，打造极致纤薄桌面体验 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- Gateron矮轴搭配低剖面双色PBT键帽，兼顾手感与耐用性 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 有线/蓝牙双模连接，支持3台设备无缝切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.1 + Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 回报率：有线1000Hz / 无线90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 配列：87键ANSI（80%） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron矮轴（北向灯位） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 键帽：低剖面双色PBT [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 电池：2000mAh，关背光续航约130小时，开RGB最低亮度约65小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 软件：支持QMK/VIA改键 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- RGB：22种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 系统：macOS/Windows/Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
- 其他：全键无冲、12个多媒体键、三档可调脚撑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k061-k1-pro-source.md)
