## 目标用户

- 追求桌面整洁与便携性的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 96%配列全尺寸布局，保留数字区与功能键，节省桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- Gateron矮轴搭配矮键帽，触发快、手感清脆，兼顾办公与游戏 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 2.4G/蓝牙/有线三模连接，多设备无缝切换，跨平台兼容 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.1 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 回报率：蓝牙模式90Hz，有线/2.4G支持全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 配列：ANSI 104键（96%） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron矮轴（North-facing） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 键帽：Low-profile double-shot PBT，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 电池：2000mAh，关背光续航约95小时，开RGB约50小时（最低亮度） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 背光：22种RGB灯效，可自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 系统：macOS / Windows / Linux 全兼容 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 尺寸：387x119mm，重750g，前高10.26mm，支持2°/4°/6°三段脚撑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
- 工作温度：-10~50℃ [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k086-k17-max-source.md)
