## 核心卖点

- 0.1mm 快速触发 + 0.2~3.8mm 可调触发行程，重新定义响应速度 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- Gateron 双轨磁轴，精准顺滑，热插拔兼容 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- Special Edition 铝合金+实木外壳，桌搭质感与性能兼得 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙 / Type-C 有线三模连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 回报率：有线及 2.4GHz 下 1000Hz，蓝牙下 125Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 配列：65% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：标准版 317.1 × 107.5 mm；特别版 319.2 × 107.6 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron 双轨磁轴，热插拔（仅兼容 Gateron 双轨磁轴） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 键帽：Cherry 高度，侧刻字符（注：规格中未明确键帽材质，此处省略） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh 锂聚合物电池，续航最高 120 小时（实验室数据） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 软件：支持 Launcher APP 自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 背光：North-facing RGB [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 声学：声音填充包（注：规格中未明确，此处省略） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 系统兼容：Windows / macOS / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
- 其他：全键无冲、三档倾斜角度（3.36° / 6.09° / 9.73°） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k134-k6-he-source.md)
