## 产品定位

为高端桌面打造的 CNC 全铝三模机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 高端办公与桌面美学用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 追求稳定无线与低延迟的电竞玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 多系统（macOS/Windows/Linux）切换的专业用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 对键盘做工与手感有极致要求的机械键盘发烧友 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 全 CNC 铝制外壳，底壳与外壳同材质，提供坚实稳重的质感与耐用性 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 三模连接（2.4GHz / 蓝牙 5.3 / Type-C），无线/有线均支持 8000Hz 回报率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 4000mAh 大电池，背光关闭续航可达 660 小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4 GHz / Bluetooth 5.3 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：8000 Hz（有线及 2.4GHz），125 Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：144.8 × 408 mm；前高 20.7 mm，后高 31.8 mm；倾斜角 5° [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Silk POM 轴，出厂润滑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 卫星轴：Screw-in PCB 卫星轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：PBT 材质（依据规格未明确，但通常为 PBT，此处不写入） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：4000 mAh 可充电锂聚合物电池 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK/VIA 改键（规格未明确，但 Keychron 产品通常支持，此处不写入） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- RGB：South-facing RGB LED [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
- 热插拔：支持 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k222-q5-ultra-source.md)
