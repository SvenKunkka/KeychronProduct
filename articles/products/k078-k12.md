## 产品定位

把紧凑便携、多设备连接和持久续航结合在一起的60%配列无线机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 便携办公与移动办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 桌面空间有限的极简主义者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 多设备办公用户（Mac/iOS/Windows/Android） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 机械键盘入门与轻游戏玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 60%紧凑配列，61键占桌面积减少40%，释放桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 4000mAh大电池，关背光续航长达240小时，无线办公一周不充电 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 蓝牙5.1三设备连接，一键切换Mac/iOS/Windows/Android [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.1 + Type-C有线，无线模式90Hz、有线1000Hz回报率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 配列：61键ANSI，12个多媒体键（Fn组合） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron G Pro机械轴/Keychron光学轴/Keychron机械轴，热插拔版本可选 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 键帽：ABS材质，OEM高度 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh锂聚合物电池，RGB背光续航72小时，白背光68小时，关背光240小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 背光：18种灯效，4级可调RGB（非背光版除外） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 系统：兼容Windows/Android/Mac/iOS [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 尺寸：塑壳版293×102mm，铝框版298×107mm，重约536g [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
- 声学：无填充（规格未提及，不写入） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k078-k12-source.md)
