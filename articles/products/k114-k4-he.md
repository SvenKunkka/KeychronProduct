## 产品定位

把磁轴性能、无线自由和木饰质感结合在一起的紧凑型键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求极致响应速度的电竞玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 看重桌面质感与木材元素的桌搭爱好者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 多平台办公（Mac/Win/Linux）且需要无线便携的用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 对磁轴技术感兴趣、想尝鲜的机械键盘发烧友 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 0.1mm 快速触发：Gateron 双轨磁轴，可调触发行程 0.2~3.8mm，为电竞带来毫秒级响应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 双模无线 + 4000mAh 大电池：2.4GHz 回报率 1000Hz，蓝牙 125Hz，续航高达 110 小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 铝壳 + 木饰特别版：标准版铝合金外壳，特别版融入木质侧边，兼顾质感与桌搭美学 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 回报率：1000Hz（有线 & 2.4GHz），125Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 配列：96% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：Standard 373 × 126 mm；Special 377 × 126 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron 双轨磁轴（仅兼容该轴体，支持热插拔） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 键帽：ABS 材质（具体高度未标注） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh 锂聚合物电池，续航最高 110 小时（实验室数据） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK / VIA（待确认，未在规格中明确） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- RGB：North-facing 背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 声学填充：未在规格中提及 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
- 系统兼容：Windows / macOS / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k114-k4-he-source.md)
