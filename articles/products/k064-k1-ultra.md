## 产品定位

把 8K 回报率性能、三模无线效率和木质工艺质感结合在一起的桌面型键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求桌搭质感与木材温润触感的桌面美学用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 需要超低延迟与长续航兼顾的无线游戏玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 多设备办公用户（macOS/Windows/Linux 三系统兼容） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 铝木融合外壳：航空级铝合金上盖搭配天然胡桃木侧边框，兼顾强度与温润质感，每一把纹理都独一无二 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 8K 回报率三模：有线与 2.4GHz 下均支持 8000Hz 回报率，响应低至 0.125ms，无线性能媲美旗舰电竞键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 自研 Milk POM 轴体：POM 材质自润滑，手感顺滑耐用，出厂预润滑，开箱即用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / Bluetooth 5.2 / Type-C 有线三模 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：8000Hz（有线 & 2.4GHz），125Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：358.6 × 128.3 mm；前高 15.44 mm，后高 19.94 mm（不含键帽）；重量 628.9 g ± 10 g [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Milk POM Switch，热插拔设计 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：ABS 材质，Cherry 高度，侧刻字符（支持 Mac/Win 双系统） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：2600mAh 锂电池，续航最长 490 小时（8K 回报率、关背光） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持 Keychron Launcher 网页驱动，可自定义键位、灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- RGB：North-Facing RGB LED，支持多种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 声学：内置消音填充，优化敲击声音 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k064-k1-ultra-source.md)
