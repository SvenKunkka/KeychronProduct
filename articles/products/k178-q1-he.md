## 核心卖点

- Gateron双轨磁轴，0.1mm灵敏度，0.2-3.8mm可调触发行程，快速触发，电竞级响应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 4-in-1动态按键，一键触发四种操作，复杂指令瞬间完成 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 全铝合金机身，CNC一体成型，重量达1735g，稳固且质感出众 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.2 / Type-C有线，三模连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 回报率：有线/2.4GHz 1000Hz，蓝牙90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 配列：75%布局，紧凑高效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron双轨磁轴，热插拔（兼容Gateron双轨磁轴） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)

- 电池：4000mAh，关背光续航可达100小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- 软件：QMK/VIA支持，全键自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
- RGB：南向RGB背光，灯效丰富 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k178-q1-he-source.md)
