## 核心卖点

- 8K回报率：有线与2.4GHz无线均支持8000Hz回报率，响应速度低至0.125ms，竞技优势显著 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 超长续航：4000mAh电池，背光关闭下续航可达660小时，RGB最低亮度下也有200小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 三模连接：2.4GHz无线、蓝牙5.3与Type-C有线，兼顾低延迟与多设备切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / Bluetooth 5.3 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：8000Hz（有线/2.4GHz），125Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：149 × 367.8 mm；前高 23.6 mm，后高 32.5 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Silk POM轴，热插拔设计 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：POM材质（Silk POM轴搭配） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh锂聚合物电池，续航660小时（背光关闭）/ 200小时（RGB最低亮度） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持QMK/VIA改键（需确认，但规格未提及，故不写） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- RGB：South-facing RGB LED，支持自定义灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 声学填充：PC定位板，螺丝固定PCB卫星轴，保证手感与声音 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS/Windows/Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k254-v3-ultra-source.md)
