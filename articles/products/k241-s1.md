## 产品定位

把航空级铝材、紧凑75%配列和低剖面手感结合在一起的超薄便携键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 追求极致轻薄便携的移动办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 桌面空间有限但追求质感的桌搭爱好者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 偏好低轴体手感与紧凑布局的机械键盘玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 需要长时间码字的文字工作者 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 对键盘做工有高要求的品质用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 全CNC铝合金外壳，一体成型，坚固耐用且质感出众 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 前高仅7.8mm，极致超薄，配合3°/6°双角度脚垫，舒适贴合 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- Gateron低剖面MX机械轴，兼顾机械手感与轻薄体验 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)

- 配列：75%紧凑布局 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 轴体：Gateron Low Profile MX Mechanical，支持热插拔 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 键帽：Double-shot PBT低剖面键帽，不透明 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 背光：North-facing RGB LED [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 定位板：钢制定位板 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 主控：超低功耗ARM Cortex-M4 32-bit STM32L432 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
- 系统兼容：Mac/Win双系统（需确认） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k241-s1-source.md)
