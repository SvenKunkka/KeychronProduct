## 核心卖点

- Keychron Apex Switch 自研轴体，出厂润滑，手感顺滑，触发灵敏 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 8K 回报率（有线/2.4G），低延迟竞技体验，蓝牙 125Hz 省电模式 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：2.4GHz / Bluetooth 5.2 / Type-C 有线三模 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 回报率：8000 Hz（有线/2.4G），125 Hz（蓝牙） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 配列：96% [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：391.29 × 140.27 mm [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Apex Switch，热插拔设计 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 键帽：ABS 材质，South-facing RGB 背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，续航最高 600 小时（8K 回报率，背光关闭） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK / VIA 改键 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k117-k4-ultra-source.md)
