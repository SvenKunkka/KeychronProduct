## 核心卖点

- 超长续航：800mAh电池，最高300小时使用时间，告别频繁充电 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k015-b5-pro-source.md)
- 多设备无缝切换：蓝牙5.2，支持macOS/iOS/Windows/Android/Linux，一键切换 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k015-b5-pro-source.md)
- 轻薄便携：前高仅9.9mm，重623g，轻松放入背包 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k015-b5-pro-source.md)

- 尺寸：437.6×120.6mm，前高9.9mm，后高14.5mm，打字角度3.2º [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k015-b5-pro-source.md)
