## 产品定位

融合磁轴性能、三模连接与铝坨坨质感的65%电竞键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Gateron双轨磁轴，支持0.2-3.8mm可调触发行程与0.1mm灵敏度，实现快速触发与动态按键4-in-1功能 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 全铝机身（外壳+定位板），兼具稳定手感与高端桌面质感 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 三模连接：2.4GHz / 蓝牙5.2 / Type-C有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 1000Hz回报率（有线/2.4GHz），蓝牙90Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 65%配列，支持全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- OSA双色注塑PBT键帽，不透明 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 4000mAh电池，关背光续航最高115小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 南向RGB背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 支持QMK改键（ARM Cortex-M4主控） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
- 兼容Mac/Win双系统 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k204-q2-he-source.md)
