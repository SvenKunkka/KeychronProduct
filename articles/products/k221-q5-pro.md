## 产品定位

把全铝机身、无线自由与 96% 配列结合在一起的旗舰型机械键盘 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 机械键盘发烧友 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- 全 CNC 铝制机身，重达 2160g，稳如磐石 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 4000mAh 大电池，蓝牙续航长达 300 小时（关背光） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- KSA 双色注塑 PBT 键帽，手感细腻耐用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙 5.1 / Type-C 有线 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 回报率：有线 1000 Hz，无线 90 Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 配列：96% 布局，保留数字区 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron K Pro 热插拔轴，兼容 3 针/5 针 MX 轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 键帽：KSA 双色注塑 PBT，不透明 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 电池：4000mAh，续航最高 300 小时（关背光） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 软件：支持 QMK/VIA，可自定义键位 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- RGB：南向 RGB LED，支持多种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 声学：PC 定位板 + 螺丝卫星轴，声音纯净 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
- 系统：兼容 macOS/Windows/Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k221-q5-pro-source.md)
