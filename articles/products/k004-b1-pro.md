## 核心卖点

- 极致轻薄设计：前高仅5.2mm，后高14.5mm，打字角度3.2°，配合425g轻量机身，打造舒适的移动办公体验 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 多设备无缝切换：蓝牙5.2连接，支持macOS、iOS、Windows、Android、Linux全系统，轻松跨平台办公 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：蓝牙5.2无线连接 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)

- 电池：800mAh锂聚合物电池，充电时间约2.5小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 键帽：ABS材质键帽，无背光 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 多媒体键：12个多媒体按键 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS、iOS、Windows、Android、Linux [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 工作环境：-10°C至50°C [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
- 产品尺寸：296 x 130 mm，重量425g [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k004-b1-pro-source.md)
