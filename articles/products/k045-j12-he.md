## 产品定位

一款兼顾办公与娱乐的 75% 磁轴键盘，适合希望体验 HE 技术，同时需要日常办公体验的用户。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)

## 目标用户

- 办公+轻游戏用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- 第一次购买磁轴键盘用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- 多设备办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Hall Effect Magnetic Switch [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- 可调触发磁轴，提供更快响应、更精准控制和更少误触。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- Instant Profile Switching [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- 独立切换 Default / Gaming / Gamepad 三种模式，一键切换工作与娱乐。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- Multi-device Workflow [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
- 支持 Mac / Windows，多设备连接，适合现代办公场景。 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k045-j12-he-source.md)
