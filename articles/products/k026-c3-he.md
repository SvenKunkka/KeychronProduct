## 目标用户

- 追求稳定输入的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 需要全尺寸布局的桌面办公人群 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Ultra-fast Lime 磁轴：快速触发，适合办公和轻游戏 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- Gasket Mount 结构：提供柔和且一致的手感，减少打字疲劳 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 全尺寸配列：保留完整功能键区，提升办公效率 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 连接方式：Type-C 有线连接，附带 Type-C 转 Type-A 适配器 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 回报率：1000 Hz [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 键帽：Cherry 高度双色注塑 PBT 键帽，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 轴体：Keychron Ultra-fast Lime 磁轴，支持热插拔（5针和3针） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 背光：North-facing RGB LED，支持多种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 系统兼容：macOS / Windows / Linux 三系统即插即用 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 工作环境：-10℃ 至 50℃ 宽温适应 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
- 倾斜角度：4°/6°/8.7° 三档调节，适配不同使用姿势 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k026-c3-he-source.md)
