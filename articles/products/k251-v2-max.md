## 目标用户

- 追求性价比的机械键盘入门用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 需要多设备切换的办公用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 喜欢紧凑布局但不想牺牲功能的桌面用户 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 轻度游戏玩家 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)

## 核心卖点

- Gasket Mount 结构，手感软弹、声音纯粹 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 4000mAh 大电池，蓝牙续航长达 190 小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 支持 2.4G / 蓝牙 / 有线三模连接，全键无冲 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)

## 其他卖点

- 1000 Hz 回报率（2.4G 和有线模式）/ 90 Hz（蓝牙模式） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 65% 配列，紧凑布局，节省桌面空间 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- Gateron Jupiter 轴体，热插拔兼容 3 针和 5 针 MX 轴 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- OSA 高度双色注塑 PBT 键帽，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 4000 mAh 电池，关背光续航约 190 小时，开 RGB 约 100 小时（最低亮度） [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 支持 QMK 改键，可通过 VIA 软件自定义 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 南向 RGB 背光，支持多种灯效 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- Gasket Mount 结构，内置消音填充，声音更纯净 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
- 兼容 Windows / macOS / Linux，附赠 Type-C 转 Type-A 适配器 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k251-v2-max-source.md)
