## 核心卖点

- 4000mAh大电池，蓝牙续航高达190小时，告别电量焦虑 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)
- Gasket Mount结构搭配PC定位板，带来柔和一致的手感与悦耳声音 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)
- 支持2.4G/蓝牙/有线三模连接，1000Hz回报率，办公游戏两不误 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)

- Double-shot PBT键帽，OSA高度，耐磨不打油 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)

- 4000mAh电池，开RGB最低亮度续航100小时，关背光续航190小时 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)
- 支持Windows/macOS/Linux系统 [已审核来源](../../external-sources/yujian-reviewed/k269-v8-max-source.md)
